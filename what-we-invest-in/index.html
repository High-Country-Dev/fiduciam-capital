Is your business a fit for Fiduciam Capital?

Here's what we look for in great companies

$5–100M in revenue

> 10% EBITDA margins

Consistent cash flow, not overly cyclical

Located in the U.S.

Longstanding operating history (10+ years)

Focus on B2B & Industrial service Industries

No Startups

No Turnarounds

No B2C Retail
